### Silicon Valley Pushes Back on Washington's Open Model Restrictions

77 companies and groups, including Google, Meta, and OpenAI, signed an open letter defending publicly downloadable AI models against premature government restrictions. The signatories argue that open models already generate one in every four AI tokens worldwide and that concentrating AI capability in a few closed systems creates single points of failure. The letter arrives as Moonshot's Kimi K3 (2.8 trillion parameters) goes public on Hugging Face. [Read more](https://images.nvidia.com/pdf/Open-Weights-and-American-AI-Leadership.pdf) [nvidia.com]
