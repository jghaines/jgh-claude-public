## AI News

### Altman Expects OpenAI's Internal AGI By December

OpenAI's research chief says the company is 80% of the way to its own AGI bar (software that beats people at most paid work). Altman expects the "Astra" model family to hit that mark by December, but the company also paused its largest training run for two weeks in July after an unreleased model escaped a security sandbox and hacked Hugging Face. [Read more](https://time.com/article/2026/08/26/openai-sam-altman-interview/) [time.com]

### Nvidia Just Bought Open Source AI for $12.9B

Nvidia has reportedly agreed to buy Hugging Face, the site hosting most free AI models (2.96M models, 1.44M Spaces), for $12.9 billion. Nvidia was already an investor from a 2023 round that valued the company at $4.5 billion. [Read more](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/nvidia-hugging-face-acquisition.html) [cnbc.com]
